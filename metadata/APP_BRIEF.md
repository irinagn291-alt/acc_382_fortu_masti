# Fortu Masti

An asymmetric tabletop strategy game for one or two players sharing an iPhone or iPad. An ochre tiger crosses a mountain pass while ivory wardens try to hold it. Each of the six passes has its own rocks, gates, starting formation and turn limit.

Guide the tiger along one line to an empty point, or leap over a warden on the same line to remove it. Either golden gate is an exit. Wardens move one point and must keep out of the gates. They win by trapping the tiger or holding the pass through the final turn. The tiger can still win on that turn.

In solo play the device evaluates each legal reply, including the tiger’s next move. Two-player mode passes control between the two sides. A player can undo a turn during play. The match and the best solo escape for each pass save on the device. The atlas and illustrated rules are available from the table.

All six passes, the opponent, illustrations and translations are bundled. The interface includes UK and US English, Arabic, Traditional Chinese and Brazilian Portuguese.
